The check (master session, 11/17)

Passed on and fixed:

- Hina gave her "You look awful. Not my turn to Heal. Eat a Noni, lunkhead." tip while she was knocked out (Stuck 18). Fixed in pygame: a fallen Hina gives no tip, and it waits for a turn she is standing.

- The booklet's battle Item line said "an item from the bag"; it now says the battle bag of the one whose turn it is, pointing to the Battle Bags section, which already explained why the Seer's Shells were missing (Stuck 12).

- The booklet said "Not everyone in the village will be glad to see Kailo digging", which read as if digging in town were allowed; it now says nobody will let Kailo dig up a village (Stuck 16). The refusal itself is ruled (a tree or a hole in town gets the village yelling).

Not problems:

- The sleeping boar's two dodges (Stuck 4): Hina's first stroke landed and woke it, as the booklet says, so it could dodge again.

- Wai'Kenpō empty in the first fight, Wave Guard later (Stuck 3): the slot it sits in opens when the taro patch is won ("Element slot unlocked"); before that it waits in the bank.

- The grocer and talk loops from mashing A, the Attack menu skipping the target pointer, and the block press that landed outside the zone (Stuck 5, 6, 7, 8, 14, 15): the player's batched presses, not the game.

- Eku and Egg (Stuck 9): different items; the item window says what the Eku is.

- Sauté weaker than a Strong stroke, the fire pit, the healing pond giving no water, "Shaka" and "Haka" (Stuck 2, 10, 11, 17): as designed and taught in the game or the booklet.

- Winning with both at 0 HP when the burned boar died (Stuck 13): the last monster fell, so the fight was won. Left as it is unless Frankie wants a ruling.
