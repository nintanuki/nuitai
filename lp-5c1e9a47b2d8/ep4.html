Design notes

1. Burning the boss in his nature stance is a great boss answer: it uses the rule taught in the first hour ("Nature heals, but it burns"), the Shell, the stances and the guard system together. When it clicked, the fight went from a wall to finished in three minutes. Large praise.

2. The Seer's Shell line "Fire and Nature, in Fire now." and the two stance icons with the live one boxed are exactly the read I asked for. "Tandayag na Opon shifts to nature stance." as text is fair warning. Large praise.

3. It took me four sessions to see that the target of the burn was the boss, not the guards. The game told me "a burning monster cannot be healed" and "burning a guard keeps him from squealing", and both are true, so I kept solving the guard. The one line that would have pointed me right is the boss shifting to nature stance, and that came only once, after more than twenty minutes. A boss whose solution only opens after twenty minutes of a heal race will lose most players before then. Large.

4. Elements belong to stances: in Lelua only Counter and Flame Kick show, in Wai'Kenpō only Wave Guard. It makes a stance switch a real choice. Nothing said this; I saw it in the menu. Praise, medium.

5. Hina's "Hand me a Noni, quick." while she is burning is wrong advice: the game then refuses the heal. A tutorial voice that is wrong once teaches me to stop listening. She should ask for Kukui when burning. Medium.

6. The guard's Truffle for 123 to 135 is about three good turns of mine in one move, and Scold's three turns barely cover Hina's long wait after it. The heal race is still the hardest part, but now I see it is meant to push you to the burn. Medium.

7. Strokes then B to get the command menu back, so Hina can build to full and Scold in the same turn: I found it by accident. If it is intended it is a powerful trick nobody told me about. Medium.

8. Spring Rain's Select window ("A warm rain over the whole party...") says what it does in words, so I could decide not to spend 14 mana on it. Praise, small.

9. "Hina is full: 30 extra HP." answers my old note about over-full HP. Praise, small.

10. "Needs Water." on grey Poi is short and enough. Praise, small.

11. Perfect blocks on a guard's blow at Hina still hurt her, and nothing on screen says why. If a block on a teammate is meant to be weaker, the meter should look different. Medium.

12. Which blows get a block meter is unclear: Gore on Hina had none and Gore on Kailo did. Medium.

13. The spur, a palm and then a stump to an empty pocket, feels like a promise the map did not keep. Small.

14. After the boss falls, the clearing's six boars stay and one ran straight into Kailo at low HP before I could move. A short safe moment after the boss would be kinder. Small.

15. The boss's end and the "slot unlock" screen went by too fast to read; the biggest win in the game so far deserves its own line held on screen. Medium.

16. The block meter rewarding mana is still the best part of defence; six perfects in a row refilled Kailo. Praise, small.

Story and characters

The story so far is a small, warm village story: a cook remaking a stew for a wake, a priestess packing for a trip, and a boar who lived on being fed. Beating the Great Boar is low key, as it should be; Kailo's "That's the last of him. The taro's safe." and Hina's "Now go and finish that stew" keep the scale human. The opening promised storms and strange wonders, and none have come yet; this was the tutorial island's last lock.

- Kailo: steady, kind, keeps his eye on the wake ("the wake won't be the same without it"). In the fight he is the shield: Wave Guard and his stance switches made him the one standing between Hina and the boss.

- Hina: still the best-written. She calls the Team Up for me ("Wait. We both have full mana..."), warns "Don't mess up your half," and nags about the stew. Her one wrong call, asking for a Noni while burning, stood out because she is usually right.

- Tandayag na Opon: less a monster than a system, as I said, and now a fair one. Fire and nature, fed by his herd, healing himself with Truffle Feast in nature, and undone by his own burn.

- Noelani, Melia, Miki, Gajumaru, Keo: not met this session. Melia's tusk is in the bag.
