What happened

- Title screen, New Game. Four intro cards (ocean world, sea monsters and storms, a cook and a priestess on an island in the northeast, "Kulaiwi Village, The last day of Summer"). No save point or save menu was met.

- Opening in Kulaiwi Village: Kailo ("Stew for the wake tonight... cakes and breadfruit for the next island"), Melia reports three boars in the taro patch, Hina tells him to do something about it, Melia is told to stay and watch the stew. After the dialogue, walking left 4 tiles moved to a market area where the boars are visible; walking down 1 and right 2 started the battle with Wild Boar A and Wild Boar B (a third boar was visible on the map but not in the fight).

- Run 1: Hina's tutorial (Yes/No on "do you remember how to fight", Light attack, stamina, mana, Strong, Sauté under Element, Defend, block meter). Kailo cooked Poi (ruined by a hit), threw a Noni at a boar. Hina was knocked out in the block round (boar hit for 71 after a 160 to 69 drop from Flambé). Wild Boar A was defeated by Kailo's Strong attack. Kailo was then knocked out (boar hit for 89, Wild Boar B still standing). Game Over at about 16:58 episode clock. Nothing dropped.

- Game Over went back to the title; Continue was greyed out (no save). New Game again, replayed the intro and tutorial (about 7 minutes of clock to reach the same fight).

- Run 2: reached the Defend step, Hina attacks, and the first block meter again at 30:00. Both at full HP, no one down. Episode ended there, in the first battle.

Stuck and confusing

1. Block meter timing. On screen: red arrows sliding left, gold zone. Narrator gave "Blow 1 in 1.10s, Blow 2 in 1.46s, Blow 3 in 1.82s". Pressed z 1.10, then 0.36, then 0.36. Hina still took 37, 54, 51 and later 71 damage; one press was reported "a miss (that press came outside the gold zone)". The instructions say the first lesson box needs z presses first, and the narrator's times changed (1.10 to 0.92 and 1.24 to 1.65) after the lesson pages closed, so the first numbers were stale. It is hard to tell which press was in the gold zone.

2. Hina's lesson pages over the block meter: three pages, each pressed z with a wait, and the timings changed after each. Confusing which numbers to use.

3. Defend lesson not obvious: after "Now Defend. Go on, humor me." it said "Highlighted: Attack" and nothing else moved. Pressing z or attacking with Hina did nothing (a long series of z presses stalled with "Waiting on a command for Hina" for about 90 seconds of clock) until left was pressed to reach Defend. The "Defend is left of your commands" line scrolled by quickly.

4. Pressing x (cancel) in the attack list did nothing during the tutorial, and up did nothing either. The Sauté was under "Element" in the stroke list rather than a COOK or Skill menu, even though the command menu has COOK and Element as separate entries.

5. Cooking Poi: "Stir!" needed up,right,down,left arrows; 19 of 20 keys were pressed but "Kailo's Poi is ruined!" came later, probably because a boar hit during the stir. Nothing in the screen said that cooking could be interrupted.

6. Noni thrown at a boar: "Kailo throws the Noni at Wild Boar B." No damage number was heard and nothing explained what a Noni does; it seems to be a throwable item with no stated use.

7. The boars are tanky and hit hard for a first fight: Wild Boar A took about 190 damage across the chain before defeat and "Flambé" and "Thorn Strike" took Hina from 160 to 0 within two blow rounds. The tutorial opponents dealt 71 and 89 damage in single blows when blocks missed.

8. Game Over sends you to the title with no save to continue from, so the whole intro and tutorial had to be replayed (about 7 minutes of clock). The intro cards cannot be skipped faster than the typing, and "z,z" presses can overshoot into the next card.

9. The narrator gave "Kailo stands at column 8, row 7" even after the camera changed to the market area; the boars and market stall people were not in "People here". It was unclear how the boar scene began; walking left 4 from the first screen triggered "SQUEEEE!" with no visible edge crossing.

10. Turn order showed "Hina, Hina, Hina" three times in a row, which looked like an error.

11. After Hina chose Yes/No, picking Yes in the second run still gave all the lessons.
