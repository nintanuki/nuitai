Mark Plays Nuitai

A blind playtest of the pygame demo by Mark, an invented professional playtester, thinking out loud. His notes follow the episode.

Mark Welcome back. Last time ended badly: Nuno sa Tulay, the little old toll keeper on the bridge north of the boardwalk, wanted 100 pule to cross, Kailo refused on principle, and the party got blown off the map. We came back at the totem in Lanu's Grove, full health, 480 pule in the pocket.

Mark What I want out of this session is simple to say. First, get past that bridge, one way or another. I predicted that paying the toll lets you cross without a fight, and I'd like to know if that's true, but I'd also like to know if the fight is winnable when I play it properly, with the right stances. Second, find out where cups come from, so the Lanu posts quest can move. Third, find the Great Boar.

Mark First, homework. Nuno's Cyclone hit Kailo for 59 "Weak!" because he was in his fire stance, Lelua. Wind beats fire. So before I go anywhere, Kailo goes into Wai'Kenpō, his water stance. It also bumps his HP from 140 to 170, which matters against a boss that hits that hard.

Mark Kailo's own pouch reads 0 of 4 now. I had it at 3 of 4 in my notes. I can't tell if I misread it last time or if the pouch emptied itself somewhere. I'll keep an eye on it.

Mark Hina's grid shows "Heal" and "ShdGr" in row 1 and an empty row B. I tried to put something into row B and nothing happened, no message, no sound. So the B row is still a mystery. I'd like the game to tell me "locked" or "nothing to place" instead of silence.

Mark Out of the grove and back into the leaf maze. The walking map tells me the little leaf creatures are called Aghoy. Last time I slipped past them. This time one walked straight into my lane, and I'd rather learn them than dodge forever.

Mark They open with a three-hit flurry, and I parried all three perfectly. That's the three-parry counter from last time, and it hit for 26. The block meter has become the part of combat I trust most: it's readable, it's fair, and it pays out.

Mark Then it turned into something else. "Aghoy B uses Shell!" "hardens against magic." Then "Shadow Guard," "A ward of light settles on Aghoy C." They don't attack much at all. They're support creatures, all three of them, buffing and healing each other.

Mark I focused Aghoy C with Kailo's strokes and it went down after about 115 damage. Good. That felt like the plan working.

Bugs

1. "Medium100%" still has no space between the stroke name and the number (stroke list, every fight, e.g. Hina vs Aghoy A, after pressing z on Light).

2. "Papanok lights the pan" / "Wild Boar B lights the pan" on Flambé: the monsters have no pan (islet Papanok fight shot-442, clearing boar fight).

3. After Hina escaped from the Aghoy fight, her face stayed at the top of the turn-order column for a moment (leaf maze, shot-324, after the Escape minigame).

4. In the Aghoy fight Kailo vanished from all eight visible turn-order slots while the Aghoy were hasted (leaf maze, around shot-308). This may be correct, but it reads like he left the fight.

5. Hina speaks in Maika's shop greeting ("My hair shines just fine on its own.") while she is knocked out (Maika's clearing, pressing z on Maika).

6. Hina showed "HP 0 of 200" after winning the clearing boar fight and stayed down, though her join message says the fallen get back up after a win (shot-2642; also seen in Episode 1).

7. The chest with Kukui showed a second "Found 'Ōlena." page that was only visible if you kept pressing z. It is easy to walk away thinking the chest held one item (boardwalk maze, top-right chest).

8. Stepping onto Maika's east path repeats her full warning every time you touch the tile, including after a walk command was sent (Maika's clearing, east exit, shot-3345).

9. In the shop the item description lagged one row behind the cursor in one frame (shot-2910 shows Kukui's text with the hook on 'Awa Tonic).

10. After paying the toll a second time, the next bump into Nuno sa Tulay opened "Fight the Nuno sa Tulay?" with Yes highlighted (toll bridge heading south, shot-3536, after "Go on, cross, before I change my mind.").

Design notes

1. Paying, fighting or "find another way around" at Nuno sa Tulay is three answers for three play styles, and the "Hehe" sells him. Good. Small.

2. The Ko'i turning last episode's grove dead end into a shortcut is excellent structure; the dead end was a promise. Large, praise.

3. Burn as the answer to healers paid off in the first boar fight, and the boars used it against me in the second. A rule that works both ways is good design. Medium, praise.

4. Pōhuehue's damage depends a lot on doing the minigame well (56 rushed, 244 clean, 134 on another try). Rewarding skill is good, but the spin phase needs a beat or a "Ready?" after the notes so the player can read it. Medium.

5. The Aghoy are a race: kill them before Haste and Green Dream, or you can't win. That's a good enemy idea, but the game never shows it. If you don't strike first it's an unwinnable seven-minute fight. Have Hina say "Don't let them get started," or don't give one encounter Shell, Haste, Heal and Green Dream all at once. Large.

6. Burn, poison, revive and party-flee cures are dropped before the fights that need them, but the "Found" text only names them. Add a one-line description to "Found" or the first-pickup message. Large.

7. Battle items come only from each hero's own pouch, and moving things there is fiddly (no confirmation message). This session it decided a fight. Either let Item reach the shared bag in battle, or have Hina point it out the first time someone is hurt with an empty pouch. Large.

8. "lights the pan" before a big fire spell is a clear warning, but the stance switch costs the turn and Papanok act several times in a row. Hina's hint system could cover it the first time: "I'm flowers, they're fire. Switch me." Medium.

9. Maika is the best-written new character: a saleswoman flirting with Hina, Hina's comeback, and a road block written as a person ("You're not quiet."). Medium, praise.

10. A save slot named "The Pork Strikes Back" is charming and costs nothing. Small, praise.

11. Nuno sa Punso fights are the right length, about a minute. Next to the boar and Aghoy fights they show the game can pace a fight well. Medium.

12. Enemy stance isn't shown on the target pointer. I had to infer it from "Weak!" or "Resisted" after the fact. Medium.

13. A "Fight the boss?" box after paying the toll should default to No. Small.

14. The item and Materials categories appear as you find things (Materials after the first chopped tree). That's good onboarding. Small, praise.

15. Fights still run long: about seven minutes for the Aghoy, seven for the first boars, five for the clearing boars. Healing enemies remain the reason. Medium.

Story and characters

The story is still mostly a small island day, and I like that: a wake, a stew, a boss boar and a toll keeper. This session added the world around the grove. Kailo is the same steady cook, a little stubborn ("This bridge is free for everyone to use" last time), but practical enough to pay. Hina had less to say in fights this time, but her answer to Maika was the best line of the session, and it fits her: proud, dry, unimpressed. Maika is new: a forest stall keeper who calls everyone lost, flirts with Hina to sell ginger, and turns Kailo back from the Lost Woods with "You're not quiet." She seems to know the forest better than anyone, and I suspect she knows more about Lanu's beasts than she says. Nuno sa Tulay turns out to be one of a kind of creature, the Nuno, with the Nuno sa Punso (of the mound) as regular enemies; he's a greedy little toll troll with a giggle, and paying him twice stings. The Child on the east pier gave the most intriguing line: a moth "as big as a house" on the mountain. The Sage's view of Lanu, and the boars as "Lanu's beasts," comes back in Maika's warning, so the Lost Woods and the Great Boar feel tied to Lanu. The themes I see are the same as before: food as care ("Kailo feeds everyone"), the grove against the village priests, and the island as a place that takes people young.
