Design notes

1. Select's help window works now and is good: plain sentences for Poi, Noni Pulu, Wave Guard, Counter, Scold, Flame Kick and Wai'Kenpō. The burn chance on Flame Kick ("30% burn") is exactly what I asked for. Praise, large.

2. Elements still do not show their mana cost in Select, though Scold does. One number would do it. Medium.

3. Heal's window says 24 and heals 36. Wrong help text is worse than none, because it teaches you not to trust the window. Medium.

4. The new "why it is grey" box ("Needs Water.", "Nobody is waiting to swap in.", "There is no running from this fight.") is short, clear and enough. It once failed to show on the first press of the session. Praise, medium; the miss is small.

5. The missing Team Up still has no reason given. With both bars full, a player looks for it, and Swap's box answers a different question. Medium.

6. The Seer's Shell turns the boss fight from guessing into reading: HP under him for the rest of the fight, and his stance icon. It is the best item in the game so far. Praise, large. But it is a drop from a monster you can easily walk past, and nothing points at it. Large.

7. You cannot see a monster's stance without the Shell, and the studio's hint "watch for when he is in his nature stance" cannot be acted on without it. A wind-up named "Bramble Charge" reads like nature and was not. Large.

8. "Weak!" and "Resisted" on damage are good feedback, but you only notice the word by its absence after seeing it elsewhere. Small.

9. The wind-up warning ("winds up for Flame Tusk!") is fair and gives you a turn. I lost Hina by ignoring it, and the game was right. Praise, medium.

10. Burn on the guards is the satisfying answer: "Wild Boar A is burning and cannot be healed", flares on every move, and it stays standing so he does not call more. It fits the stance and heal rules exactly. Praise, large. The game never suggests burning the guards instead of the boss; I found it at minute 28 of my third session. Medium.

11. The Great Boar's heal rate (a 135 Truffle against about 100 a round from the party) is still the wall. With the Shell's numbers I can now see it is a race you can't win by damage, which is at least honest. Large.

12. Exploring paid off fast: in five minutes, a Seer's Shell, two Life Totems, a bow, a coconut, wood. The island is dense and rewards poking around. Praise, large. The story only points at the boss, though. One line from Hina or the lodge ("you'll want a Shell for him") would steer without holding hands. Medium.

13. The adze gathering (a 10-chop count, wood, a coconut for the stew, a stump ambush) ties the tool, the stew quest and combat together neatly. Praise, medium.

14. The healing pond one room before the boss is generous and well placed. Praise, small.

15. The bag's new icons and categories (Materials, Special) make it much easier to read. Praise, small. "Found Hamaya" still says nothing until you open the bag. Small.

16. The Equip compare screen, with gains in yellow and losses in red, is clear. Praise, small.

17. The save slot named "The Pork Strikes Back" is a nice bit of personality. Praise, small.

18. Item cannot target a fallen member and gives no reason; the pointer just skips her. Small.

19. On the studio's notes: the squeal change works as described; with a boar standing he never squealed. The burn change works, and is the key to the fight. The sleeping-hit change works. I agree with every change. I still disagree a little with "There is more of the island" being only in the studio's notes: in the game, nothing said it.

Story and characters

The story stood still this session; it was a fight and a walk. The world got richer in small ways. The grove villager's "Lanu grew the knees, she can listen to them complain" makes Lanu a "she" and a goddess people joke with, which says a lot about the faith here: close, practical, affectionate. "Another cup left out for Lanu" on the post near the pond says the same in Kailo's voice. The save title, "The Pork Strikes Back", shows the game does not take the boar too seriously, even while it beats me.

- Kailo: still warm and practical. His line at the lanu post is small and good. In fights he is the one who carries the damage and the stances.

- Hina: "That's him. Are you ready?" She leads into the boss. She falls fast to fire, which makes protecting her the whole job, and that fits a priestess who scolds everyone but needs a guard.

- Tandayag na Opon: now a known quantity, 540 HP and fire, fed by his herd. Less a monster than a system.

- The old man on the dock: seen, not met. Someone on the far shore too.

- The Aghoy: green fairies, small and tough, and the source of the Seer's Shell.
