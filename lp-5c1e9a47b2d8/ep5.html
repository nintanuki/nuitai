Design notes

1. Hina's single praise line, "Fine. That was good. Don't make it weird.", works. Once is funnier and less noisy than every time. Praise, small.

2. Burn stopping heals keeps working on every new family: "Taong Tuod A is burning and cannot be healed." on the stumps' Sap Mend. One rule, many enemies. Praise, large.

3. Papanok C "shifts to fire stance" right after being burned in nature. An enemy that reacts to your plan feels smart, and the burn still flares through it. Praise, medium.

4. When a target dies mid-string, the next stroke goes to the next enemy on its own. Good. Praise, small.

5. The bounty counter on the after-fight page ("Boars 2/10.") is exactly the right feedback in the right place. Praise, medium.

6. The bounty payout has no acknowledgement. "That's the lot. Here's your 500 pule." after a 27-minute boss fight. One line from the Trapper or a hunter about the boar would make the win land. Medium.

7. Melia's tusk is set up in Episode 1 and cannot be paid off: she still asks for it with the tusk in the bag. If this comes later, she should at least react to the boar being dead. Large.

8. Nobody in the village or the house mentions the Great Boar being dead. The boar was the island's problem; the island should notice. Medium.

9. A dark portrait in the shop is the only warning that nobody can use an item. It is correct but too quiet: I spent 40 on a spear neither of them can hold. A word ("No one can use this") or a greyed price would stop it. Medium.

10. "Bracer.", "Sash." and "Garland." are not descriptions. Every other item gives stats or an effect. Medium.

11. The Pearl Anklet names shadow and spirit, elements I have never met. That is a fine tease. Praise, small.

12. The new third slot in row 1 is a good quiet reward, but the screen that announced it after the boss went too fast to read. I found it by opening the Element screen. Small.

13. Kailo's Status page shows "EXP to next Vitality 88/100". Per-stat experience is a good idea that the victory screen should show, not only a green arrow. Small.

14. Gardens: Hina's "Don't leave it bare. It should be replanted. I'll show you how." is the game's best kind of teaching, in her voice, at the moment. Praise, large.

15. But a seed's text says "Plant it with HINA." and pressing A on it in the bag does nothing, with no box. The studio's "why it is grey" box would solve it: "Plant it at a bare patch." Medium.

16. Grocer seeds ('Uala, Akai) that match the stew list tie the shop, the garden and the quest together. Praise, medium.

17. Noelani's "tell her I did her chores again" and Melia's "I am doing my chores! I'm counting the pots." answer each other across the village. Cheap and very good. Praise, medium.

18. This side of the island is now a formality in combat: strokes into Flame Kick ends nearly every fight. That is right after the boss, but the island has no new challenge left on it, which tells me it is time to leave it. Medium (pacing).

19. Three found items in a row named only ("Found Cure.", "Found Kukui.", "Found 'Awa Tonic.") with no hint. The bag descriptions are good ("Kava tonic. Heals one member 80 HP. Calming."), but I had to go to the bag to know I had an element. Small.

Story and characters

The story is between chapters. The boar is dead and paid for, and the game has quietly shifted to home: the stew, the garden, Noelani's list, Melia's chores. Old Pono's wake is still the clock, though nothing has said how much time is left. The dock, the man on it, Miki's brother, Hina's trip and the Callers in Makua are all hanging, which is the right number of threads for an opening island. What is missing is the island reacting to the boar: nobody has said thank you or even "is it true?".

- Kailo: still the same kid after a boss, "I'll take my sandals off. ...wait we don't wear sandals." He calls Noelani "ma'am" and does what he is told in his own house. I like that he did not change.

- Hina: the best character still. She teaches gardening in her own voice, "Don't leave it bare.", and is impatient about it, "Let's go do something else instead of standing here waiting." Her single dodge praise, "Don't make it weird.", is perfect for her. She can only plant because she is a Māla priestess, I assume; māla is a garden, so her title fits now.

- Melia: a kid who skips chores and lies about it cheerfully. She has not noticed the boar is dead, which is either a missed hook or a setup.

- Noelani: runs the house and does Melia's chores too, "She owes me." She looks after both kids. I still do not know who she is to Kailo, but she reads like an aunt or a guardian, not a mother.

- Miki: unchanged; still waiting for word of her brother.

- The Trapper: business only. Pays and posts the next job.

- Keo: on the beach, did not talk to him this time.
