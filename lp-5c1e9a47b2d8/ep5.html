Bugs

1. The player's walking sprite on the map is the black-haired girl with a red flower (Hina's look), although the narrator and every text box say Kailo leads; seen the whole episode, for example in Kailo's House (shot-1457) and in the village (shot-1414).

2. Noelani's "Still missing" count did not change after "Give her what you have so far?" handed over the Pork, Eggs, 'Awapuhi and the Coconut: it read "4 Fish, 2 Coconut, 2 'Uala, 2 Akai Lau and 1 Māla Shoyu" before and after (Kailo's House).

3. In the three-boar fight in the boar maze, "Wild Boar B falls" was shown, but later the narrator listed "Wild Boar B: standing" again and the turn bar lost its slot, which greyed out the Combo for no visible reason (right after the Light Wave fight).

4. The text box of an NPC or chest reopens when confirm is pressed once too many (beach Villager, Keo, the pond forest chest); one confirm closes the box and the next press talks again. Small, but easy to hit.

5. Chopping a palm: the narrator counts "chop 10 of 10" and "chop 5 of 5" on the stump but no item message ever appeared, so the player cannot tell what the tree gave (pond forest, near the Lanu post).

6. In water stance the Element list shows only "Counter" and the cursor starts on a stance row, so pressing down then confirm blind switches the stance and spends the turn instead of casting (tree room fight).

7. The grocer's "How many x1" box had no total cost or owned count, and my 'Uala purchase gave no message; only the Akai purchase showed "Bought 3 Akai Seed." (shot-1307, 988 pule for six seeds).

8. The Kulāiwi Forest sign is used for the crossroads and the pond forest, and Kulāiwi Village for three different screens; the names cannot tell rooms apart.

Design notes

1. The stance decides the move set: in water, Kailo's Element list is only Counter, in fire it has Sauté, and max HP changes (220 in water, 180 in fire). A good idea (a stance is a role) but not announced when you pick it. Medium.

2. The Combo adjacency rule (both heroes at seven marks, next to each other on the turn bar) makes combos a puzzle about turn order. When it lined up, Tinju Surya did 176 and felt great. Praise. Large.

3. The three combo minigames (lights, circle punch, stir) are good variety, but the lights game starts while the string is still shown, and Light Wave's result is unreadable. Medium.

4. The parry meter with the narrator's timing is fair, and three perfect parries made Kailo's counter finish an Aghoy. Praise. Small.

5. Teaching has moved into the world: Tutori in the Chief's Hut covers Select, stances, the grocer, the lodge, Ama and the school. The right shape, but I found it with a few minutes left. It should be pointed to by Noelani or the quest log. Large.

6. Noelani's question box and the hand-over make the stew list a good tracker; it should be mirrored in the quest log. Small.

7. Planting seeds in the taro plots, with Hina saying "It takes a while to grow. Let's go do something else instead of standing here waiting." teaches waiting well. The follow-up "It is still growing." gives no sense of how long. Small.

8. Melia's scene is the best writing so far: the frog joke, the prize, then a sudden question about the father and Kailo's "...". Praise. Large.

9. Preemptive strikes (the spiral wipe when you touch an enemy first) are a good reward for care. Praise. Small.

10. Boar healing: one boar is always asleep recovering 15 a turn, another throws truffles for 30, another heals 37; three-boar fights run six to ten minutes for stat gains only. Large.

11. Hina's kit is thin: Light strokes of 4 to 5 damage and one Shadow Guard element; she depends on Kailo for her damage. Medium.

12. Item pouch: after two Noni Pulu were used in a fight, the pouch showed 0 of 4 while the rest of the Noni Pulu sat in the shared bag. Medium.

Story and characters

Kailo is a warm, practical cook; his sandals joke with Noelani shows he still plays the child to her. Melia says the unsayable and then apologises; her question about the father tells the player more than any quest text, and Kailo's silence says the rest. Hina is on a path to a promotion in the village priests' order, which Chief Ulu calls a pilgrimage; this explains her trip and sets up a clash with the Sage's view of Lanu. Noelani keeps the books for the house and the stew. Keo already knew Kailo beat the Great Boar. Chief Ulu is kind, invites Kailo to bring the stew to Old Pono's wake, and does not mention the boars. Tutori is a funny child with a grandfather and a talent for knowing things; the game uses him as its help desk. The teacher on the beach drills Lelua, "a martial art from the Fire Islands", which ties Kailo's fire stance to a place. The Sage was not seen this time.
