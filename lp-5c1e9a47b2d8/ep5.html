Stuck and confusing

1. The grove's east door again. Took it expecting to reach the glade post. It put him in the walled bottom-left nook of Maika's glade (shot-318). He'd written the warning down last episode. The narrator listed the open edges as south, west and east, but the post area can't be reached from the nook. He walked back west into the grove, so the door isn't one-way.

2. Bag assignment. In battle, Item showed only "Noni Pulu x3" for Kailo, with no Seer's Shell and no Life Totem. The field menu shows "Bag 3/4" with no hint how to fill it. Picking an item in Items gives "Use" and "Give", and Give is how. Nothing in the game teaches this.

3. Target pointer default. Hina at 3 HP used her own Noni Pulu. The pointer started on Kailo, Mark pressed z, and "Hina gives Kailo the Noni Pulu." She fell on the next enemy turn.

4. Pouch cap. After the Nuno, pule read exactly 1000 ("Leaf Pouch, Holds 1,000 pule"). Nothing said money was lost. Only Noelani mentions it ("it's stuffed").

5. Slot unlock screen skipped. After the Nuno, mashing confirm closed a "slot unlock" screen he never read (the narrator only reported "Screen closes: slot unlock").

6. Give moved the hook. Giving the Life Totem to Hina left the hook on Hina, and the next Give (a Seer's Shell meant for Kailo) went to her too.

7. L and R in the Items menu sometimes didn't flip pages, for example while the "HINA can't learn this." message was up.

8. Quest text: "water the five posts in the grove", but the posts are on different screens around the forest.

9. Green Aghoy revives. "Green Dream" (sprout icon) revives the Aghoy when it falls. One got back up three times in one fight. No way to clear the sprout that he found.

10. Pule after a fight once read 945 on the victory screen and 948 on the save screen a minute later.

11. The boar maze west gap and lodge route need a palm-row detour every time. Twice his walk was blocked by a palm he'd misread as open ground.

Design notes

1. Pōhuehue is still the best thing in the game. Five notes and a spin that ends at the top gave 271, 397 and 210. Every part of the input shows up in the number. Large, praise.

2. Bag assignment is hidden. Use/Give sits one menu deep and nothing teaches it. Two fights now have turned on it (the Life Totem in Episode 3, Hina's Noni here). A one-line Hina tip the first time a battle bag is empty would fix it. Large.

3. The target pointer should start on the user, or on the most hurt member, when a healing item is used. Medium.

4. The pouch cap should warn when it eats money, at least "Your pouch is full" on the victory screen. Small to medium.

5. The Seer's Shell is excellent once you use it: both stances, current stance, HP bar and mana debt in one line. But it took four episodes to learn what it does. The description belongs where you first pick it up. Medium.

6. The stance and weakness puzzle on the Nuno worked this time. Opening stance weak to the fire/flower Combo, spirit stance with a water cyclone that hits fire Kailo "Weak!". Cyclone All's text ("Water magic") confirmed it after the fact. Better signposting mid-fight would help (Hina could name the element). Medium, mostly praise.

7. The bridge choice menu (pay, refuse on principle, go around) is a real role-playing choice. Paying the toll and the 600 bounty pull in different directions. Small, praise.

8. "Hunt done: the toll Nuno. Back to the lodge." on the after-fight page is a great reminder. Small, praise.

9. Green Aghoy self-revive (Green Dream) is readable through the sprout icon, but a regular fight that revives three times drags. Medium.

10. The Seeds page, the Eku comparison and the per-stat arrows in Equip are clear and nice to read. Small, praise.

11. Totem revive ("The fallen are back on their feet.") works, but it means a long walk home after any knockout. Small.

12. The text gags (sandals, "This is robbery", Gajumaru's slow speech) carry long stretches of travel. Medium, praise.

13. Four-boar packs with truffle-throwing take a long time without a multi-target answer. Medium.

- Hina: coaches mid-fight ("It's not my turn to Heal yet, and I'm hurt. Use a Noni Pulu on me, quick."). Has feelings about the Lost Woods ("And you know how I feel about that."). New thread. Her house is all packed bags.

- Noelani: "Don't track mud in, Kailo. I just swept." Holds pule, sells a bigger wallet, "I won't even spend it. Much." Eight going on forty.

- Gajumaru: a turtle person from the Kame Kingdom. "I will... be... right... here..." His favorite again.

- The Trapper: "E komo mai! What'll it be?" Paid out cleanly.

- Maika: gone to dig roots "where the trees are thickest". "Don't follow." That's the Lost Woods, per Hina.

- Nuno sa Tulay: a proper boss with a two-stance puzzle. Beaten.

- Kailo's dad: still away, but now there's physical evidence. The oar.

- The child: still the house-sized moth on the mountain.

Review so far

Nuitai is a small-island RPG about a cook and a priestess, where the battle system carries the culture. Stances are the characters' faiths and kitchens, offerings to the god are how you save and heal, and every monster is a piece of Pacific or Philippine folklore with its own rhythm. This episode it clicked. The Nuno sa Tulay fight that beat me two episodes ago became a fair puzzle once I read his stances, saved first and kept the Combo for the right moment, and winning felt earned. The Combo minigame, parry lanes and turn bar are the best parts. The writing is warm and funny, especially Noelani, Hina and Gajumaru.

What doesn't work is the menus and the teaching. Bag assignment, the Seer's Shell, the pouch cap and the slot-unlock screen are all things the game knows and never tells you, and two fights have turned on that. The map leans on palm walls that look like paths, and the quest text sends you to the wrong place. Long regular fights (revive Aghoys, four-boar packs) start to drag.

Would I recommend it today? Yes, to someone who likes Chrono Cross style systems and doesn't mind poking at menus. With a few lines of teaching, I'd recommend it to anyone.

My notes for next time

- Where I am: boar maze (two screens south of the Crossroads), 1980 pule, Kailo 130/150, Hina 153/190. NOT SAVED since the village totem (slot 1, 1:27, 1000 pule). Save first. Nearest totems: the village east screen (south through the palm maze) or Lanu's Grove (north two mazes).

- Bags: in Items, pick an item, then pick a member, then "Use" or "Give". Give puts it in that member's battle bag (4 slots each). Hina's bag: Life Totem ("Raises one fallen member to 50% HP"). Kailo's: Noni Pulu. Watch where the hook lands after a Give.

- Seer's Shell: "Scans one enemy in a fight." Shows both stances, current stance, HP and mana debt. Keep one in each bag.

- Nuno sa Tulay: beaten. Opening stance is weak to Pōhuehue (397). Spirit stance uses lightning (jumps) and a water cyclone ("Weak!" on fire Kailo). Kailo's pan is resisted in Wai'Kenpō. Drop: Cyclone All, "Water magic on all enemies", which neither of them can learn.

- Combo: right on the command menu when both are at 7 marks and side by side on the turn bar. Highlighting Combo previews the new turn order.

- Purple name plus a guarding pose means guarding (Hina's name was purple). That answers the old "Kailo's name went purple".

- Green Aghoy: "Green Dream" puts a sprout icon on it, and it revives with 28 to 55 HP when killed. "Hardens against magic" is a blue shield icon. Pan it.

- Boars: Sauté to burn the healer, then pan. Truffles heal 30 to 37, and boars throw truffles at each other.

- Money: Lauhala Bag holds 5,000 (bought from Noelani for 800). Noelani also holds pule ("Can you hold some pule for me?").

- Lodge: Trapper shop and hunts (Great Boar 800, Six Aghoy, Walking Corpse 1200). Sells Hamaya (bow), Hula Ki'i, Ihe, Kagura Fan, Kapa Wraps, Tokotoko, Ananá set, Pearl Anklet. Buys wings 60, hides 50, elements 70. Gajumaru plays Kaigara: "I will... be... right... here..."
