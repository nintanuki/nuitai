- Cast page: Tawiri the Caller (Shadow and Spirit, Hula Ki'i, skill Call) is a party member who will join later; Kailo's page says he feeds everyone after a battle and Hina's page says anyone who fell gets back up with 1 HP after a win. In the game Hina stayed at 0 HP after a win. Disagreed.

- Hina's page: "Only Hina can replant a garden." Matches earlier episodes.

- Controls: B swings the lead fighter's weapon and starts a battle with that blow landed (confirmed: "Along the top: Preemptive strike!"). SELECT in a fight reads a command; Tutori said the same.

- Getting Started: save only at an Akua totem, field menu has no Save (matches). The booklet says three save slots; the game offered three and prompted "Save over slot 2?".

- Look-ups on purpose: none were done during this episode.
