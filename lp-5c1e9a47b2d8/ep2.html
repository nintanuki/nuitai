Chopping a tree in the village now gets a telling off, as digging does. That is consistent, and consistency is what I wanted. Design notes

1. Hina's element-row lesson ("Row 2 is twice as strong as row 1", "Put what you use most where you can pay for it") is clear, short and in her voice. Praise, medium. But it only triggers when you open the Element screen; after a whole first session I had never opened it, and that one screen roughly doubled my damage. It should be prompted after the first fight or by a line from Hina before the boss. Large.

2. Teaching an element from the bag through a two-line scene ("...that's right, I can't read" / "Here, give it to me, I'll read it for you.") is the best piece of character-through-menu writing so far. Large praise.

3. Team Up: triggered by full mana on both and adjacency in the turn order, announced by Hina the moment it becomes possible, and shown by an icon pair. A rule you can see on screen and plan for. Large praise. Its conditions after a stance change are not shown; when it is not available the menu should say why. Medium.

4. Overheal from food ("Hina is full: 40 extra HP") turns Cook into a pre-buffer before big hits. Good idea that fits Kailo. Praise, medium. Display "200/160" could use a different colour so it does not read as a bug. Small.

5. Perfect blocks refill mana. Defence feeds elements and Scold, so the block meter matters to offence. Praise, medium.

6. Costs are still invisible in battle. Scold took 7 (all of it), Poi took 7, Heal row 2 took 2, Flame Kick row 2 took 2; I learned every number by watching the bar. Holding Select did nothing that I could see. Put the cost beside each command, or make the Select hold obvious. Large, since this was in my last notes too.

7. The Great Boar fight is a heal race the player loses by default. Each guard Truffle heals 97 to 135, roughly two to three full player turns of damage; Squeal brings guards back even when one is alive. Even with every block perfect, row 2 elements, Scold, a Team Up and water stance, I could not get him under 60 percent in about sixteen minutes of fight. The studio says it is meant to be hard and to come back stronger; if so, the game needs to show where "stronger" comes from, because from here every route goes back to areas already cleared. Large.

8. Hina's mid-fight coaching on water stance and Wave Guard ("I'm hurt, you're fine. Switch to Wai'Kenpō. Water guards." / "The yellow box by your name is on water now.") is the right tone and timing. Praise, medium. It arrived only at 25 minutes into the fight; if she had brought it up at the start of the boss, the fight's shape would have been clear earlier. Medium.

9. Greyed options with no reason: Poi greyed at full mana, Team Up absent, Swap greyed. A one-line reason under the menu would remove guesswork. Medium.

10. The Frying Pan's stat preview (white, red and yellow arrows, before and after) is clear and quick. Praise, small. The description "Raises Strength but lowers Perception" leaves out the HP and Agility changes the preview shows. Small.

11. Weapon Mastery bars per weapon type are a good long-term hook. Praise, small.

12. Chapter title on the save slot ("The Pork Strikes Back"). Praise, small.

13. Burn is a chance with no number on screen. If burn is the counter to healing, its odds should be visible. Medium.

14. The turn order sometimes not showing Hina at all for eight slots made planning around her impossible. Either a bug or a speed issue; either way confusing. Medium.

15. Studio notes check-in: Poi on the Cook list works (fixed). Hold Select for cost: not seen working. "I can't Heal yet" meaning not-her-turn: in my session it appeared with 0 mana, so the wording still misleads.

Story and characters

The story did not move this session; all of it was the boar woods and the Great Boar's clearing. What moved was character through systems.

- Kailo: the line "...that's right, I can't read" adds a real detail. A cook who works from memory and hands, not recipes. It fits his father being absent and nobody teaching him letters. It is played light, and Hina does not mock him for it, which says something about her too.

- Hina: still the best-written character. She reads the scroll for him without a joke, she coaches the stance switch with "Do it.", and she grumbles "It's only half, don't whine." She is the tactician of the pair and the game uses her as the tutorial without making her feel like one. Her packing for a trip is still unexplained.

- Tandayag na Opon: now clearly the boss of a herd, not a lone brute. He fights by calling and being fed, which suits Melia's line that he is "too fat to come get it himself." That joke turned out to be a design.

- Melia, Keo, Noelani, Miki, Gajumaru: not seen this session.
