What happened

- Opened mid-fight on the beach with Hina's lesson box up. Adam blocked all three blows of the first meter with perfect timing (press z 1.09, then 0.23, 0.23 seconds after the narrator's times). Hina: "Fine. That was good. Don't make it weird."

- Hina's strokes did 2 damage each while the boars "brace for the blow." Kailo's Sauté did 22 to 25 against both boars (shown "Weak!"). Wild Boar A fell to a 41 from a block counter. Wild Boar B shifted to fire stance, then Sauté did 6 to 12 on it; it burned and fell. Hina healed Kailo for 18 on her turn. Drops: Roast Pork, Noni Pulu, 120 shells. A slot-unlock screen said "Element slot unlocked."

- After the fight: Kailo and Hina talked with Melia about the stew being ruined, the boss boar, and the wake for Old Pono. Hina left ("I'll take my cake now", "you know where I live"). Kailo: "I should head home first, maybe Noelani can help."

- Talked to a Villager ("They say the Callers in Makua talk to the dead.") and the Grocer, who runs a shop (Breadfruit 10, Noodles 10, Pia 8, Pineapple 10, Salt 2, Taro 8, Wheat 8, Akai/Taro/'Ula seeds 2). Bought 3 Taro with 120 shells.

- Went north into Kulāiwi Forest (music "Potions, Poisons and Forest Mushrooms"). Fought a Wild Boar alone (preemptive strike, lots of Sauté, it used Heal for 37 three times, Kailo wasted a Noni by mis-navigating the menu, Poi was ruined by a hit); it fell to burn. Next forest room: fought one Taong Tuod (it used Blind on Kailo; it burned to death). Fought two Papanok (chickens; weak to fire, they shift to fire stance and use Flambé and Sauté for up to 42 damage) in the east grass area; Kailo ended at 4 HP after a third Papanok and survived. Escape failed ("Kailo can't get away!"). Drops included Egg, Noni, Taro, shells; STR and AGI went up. Money ended at 346 shells.

- Walked north to a room with Aghoy (green leaf birds). Preemptive-free fight began at 17 HP: Aghoy B used Green Dream on Aghoy A ("will rise again if they fall"). Aghoy A fell at 29:59 with Aghoy B still standing. Episode ended mid-fight at 17 of 130 HP.

- A Sirena A was seen in the water of the east grass room but not fought. No save point was found.

Stuck and confusing

1. Kailo's command menu keeps its cursor on the last used row (Cook or Element), and pressing "down,down" from there sent Adam to the wrong menu: a Noni was used by mistake at near full health, Poi was grayed out and keys were wasted, and Item was not reachable with one more down press (it wrapped to Attack). Narrator said "Highlighted: Cook" or "Highlighted: Poi (grayed out)".

2. Poi was grayed out later in battle with no message saying why (probably no Water in the bag).

3. A Sauté from a Papanok did 42 damage and dealt with no block meter, so there was no way to prevent it.

4. Block meter timing: the narrator's "reaches the middle in S" values changed after the first press (1.27, then 0.23, 0.23). A press sent with a wrong time (0 or 0.5) counted as a miss for blow 1 once.

5. Early walk after the fight: "walk up 2" did nothing until the text box (Kailo: "I should head home first, maybe Noelani can help.") was closed with z; walk commands return unchanged positions while a box is open.

6. The shop's quantity prompt: pressing "up,up" after selecting Taro at quantity 1 gave an unclear number; unknown how many were bought (bag later showed Taro x4).

7. Strokes menu: each z press adds a stroke with no clear ending of the turn; stamina bars on the right shrink and Strong turns gray.

8. The field menu items page says Roast Pork "Recovers 5 HP." but pressing z on it does nothing (it is an ingredient).

9. The forest's north pocket in the second room: Adam was boxed into a corner of palm trees; "walk left" said "something solid."

10. The game is unclear on how Hina left the party, and no way to get her back was found.

11. One wrapper note: the duplicate [shot-219], [shot-429] and [shot-990] tags are in this transcript where a new screenshot was not taken.
