Sound effects

Every sound effect the game uses, with what it is used for now. Press play to hear one.

Plain attacks

One of these four is picked at random for every plain physical attack, whoever swings it.

PunchIn the plain-attack pool; also a monster's blow, a poison tick and anything with no sound of its own.
BladeIn the plain-attack pool.
Blunt thumpIn the plain-attack pool.
ThrownIn the plain-attack pool.

Spells and elements

A spell plays the magic zap, then its element's sound a moment later. A plain attack that carries an element plays only that element's sound after the blow.

Magic zapThe first half of every spell that hits; plain attacks no longer use it.
Afi, fireAfter a fire hit.
Wai, waterAfter a water hit.
Lau, natureAfter a nature hit.
La, lightAfter a light hit.
Ata, shadowAfter a shadow hit.
Mana, spiritAfter a spirit hit.

Healing and misses

SparkleHealing and buffs landing; also a good result in some small games, such as cooking or a bite while fishing.
WhooshA blow that misses or is dodged.

Menus

Menu moveMoving the cursor; also a good press in some timed games.
Menu selectChoosing something; also a so-so press on the block meter.
Can'tA dull bump for what you cannot do; also a wrong press in a timed game.

Timed moments

ClankA good, not perfect, timed block: a weapon caught on a shield. No word shows with it.
PerfectA perfect timed block or a perfect press in a timed game.
Stat blipOne stat point ticking up on the victory screen.

Chest openingOpening a chest.

Hina's scold

The babbling voice of Hina's Scold: no words, only a squeaky chatter that rises and falls like a scolding. Nagging Mumble plays for her Scold. The other three are unused for now.

Squeaky ChatterUnused for now: high, bright blips, fast, two short sentences that rise and drop.
Nagging MumblePlays for her Scold: lower and softer, vowel-like "ah" and "eh" sounds that wobble, two sentences run together.
Huffy Tut-TutUnused for now: sharp puffs with a blip after each, three bursts, then a low "hmph".
Rapid LectureUnused for now: tiny quick blips, three sentences at full speed, each snapped shut.
