The author's own work

The world of Nuitai: its names, places, peoples, gods, history and rulings, the rules of the battle game, the story scene, the drawn maps, and the six element icons in the battle test, drawn in Aseprite. All rights reserved.

Real-world cultures inspired the world, among them Polynesian, Southeast Asian, Indian and Japanese sources. The gods, peoples and words here are the author's own inventions, even where they borrow a real name or idea, and they do not describe any real faith or people.

Games named as inspirations, such as Chrono Trigger, Final Fantasy and Dragon Quest, belong to their owners. They are named, not copied: no art, music or screenshots from them appear on this site.

How AI was used

The world is the author's. Every place, people, god, name, date and rule in the wiki was decided by the author, in notes, conversations and a numbered log of rulings, more than 180 so far. Claude does the clerical work: it sorts those facts into articles, writes them up in a plain encyclopedia voice, links each name to its page, and checks that the articles agree with each other. When Claude suggests something, it becomes part of Nuitai only if the author decides it, and then it is logged as the author's ruling. The wiki is a record of the author's world, not creative writing by an AI.

  • The illustrations are AI placeholder concept art, made with Google Gemini and a few with ChatGPT. They stand in until the author draws the real ones, and they are labeled as placeholders wherever they appear.
  • The code for the battle, the story, the wiki and this site was written with Claude, to the author's design.

Under current United States Copyright Office guidance, a picture made entirely by a generator is not protected by copyright, so nobody owns these pictures, the author included. The world they illustrate is the author's.

It is argued after a complaint, not granted before one, so this site is arranged not to need it. Everything here is the author's own, licensed for reuse, or free to use.
