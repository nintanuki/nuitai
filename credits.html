Credits and legal

Nuitai is a hobby project, shared by link with friends and family. Nothing here is sold and nothing makes money. This page says who made what, what may be reused, and how AI was used.

The author's own work

The world of Nuitai: its names, places, peoples, gods, history and rulings, the rules of the battle game, the story scene, the drawn maps, the six element icons in the battle test, drawn in Aseprite, and the music on the front page and the games' title screens, Main Theme, the author's own composition. All rights reserved.

Real-world cultures inspired the world, among them Polynesian, Southeast Asian, Indian and Japanese sources. The gods, peoples and words here are the author's own inventions, even where they borrow a real name or idea, and they do not describe any real faith or people.

Games named as inspirations, such as Chrono Trigger, Final Fantasy and Dragon Quest, belong to their owners. They are named, not copied: no art, music or screenshots from them appear on this site.

How AI was used

The world is the author's. Every place, people, god, name, date and rule in the wiki was decided by the author, in notes, conversations and a written list of decisions, 340 of them today. Claude does the clerical work: it sorts those facts into articles, writes them up in a plain encyclopedia voice, links each name to its page, and checks that the articles agree with each other. When Claude suggests something, it becomes part of Nuitai only if the author decides it, and then it is written down as the author's decision. The wiki is a record of the author's world, not creative writing by an AI.

  • The illustrations are AI placeholder concept art and will be replaced. Nearly all were made with Google Gemini, a few with ChatGPT, and each picture says which under it. They stand in until the author draws the real ones.
  • The SNES Demo's faces are cut down from the Gemini pictures, and four of its six small element icons were placed by Claude from the author's icons.
  • The code for the battle, the story, the wiki and this site was written with Claude, to the author's design.
  • Ask Sarjana is answered by Claude from the wiki's articles, and can be wrong. The wiki is the record.
