The rules

The wait after a move is the only cost, as on the battle page with Stamina switched off.

  • An autopilot plays the party, not a person. It heals anyone under 45% HP, guards once someone is hurt, throws statuses at big enemies, and otherwise uses whatever hits hardest for its wait. A person would play better, so read the numbers as rough.
  • Every number in the game is still a placeholder. This shows the shape: who carries, who struggles, which fight is too hard.
  • Kiki's Mix is not built yet, so she fights with her shared moves only.
  • A turn is one fighter acting, party or enemy. A fight still going after 400 turns counts as a stalemate.
  • Swapping, when it is on, follows four rules, at most one swap a turn: bring in Hina when someone is down, bring in a healer when someone is hurt and nobody on the field can heal, step out when nearly down, and step out for someone on the bench who hits at least half again as hard against these enemies. It never sends away the only healer on the field.
