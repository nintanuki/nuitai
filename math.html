Battle math

The numbers under the battle, for anyone who wants to know why a move worked or didn't. Every number here is read from the same file the game reads, so it is always the one the game uses.

How a chance works: the game picks a random number from 0 up to 1, and compares it with the chance. A 15% chance means the random number has to land under 0.15.

ChancesHow hardOver time

Chances11

Each one is a roll of the random number.

Scold fails, in the browser battle25%
The chance Hina's Scold does nothing in the browser battle. In the cartridge game Scold goes by Willpower instead (below).random < scold_fail_chance: the scold fails
Scold and Charm, at least10%
Hina's Scold and Maika's Charm take hold by Willpower: the user's Willpower out of theirs and the monster's together. Even Willpower is half the time. Never less than this. Bosses are never scolded or charmed.chance = mine / (mine + theirs), never below will_least
Scold and Charm, at most90%
Never more than this.chance never above will_most
Kill15%
The chance Kill drops a monster outright. On a boss it never rolls; it just fails.random < kill_chance: the monster drops
A status taking hold75%
Poison, sleep, blind, slow and the rest land this often. Otherwise the target resists.random > status_hit_chance: resisted
Blind missing50%
A blind fighter's physical attack misses this often.blind and random < blind_miss_chance: miss
Catching fire10%
An Afi move sets the target burning this often at tier 1. Each tier above 1 adds the next number. The table below shows every tier.random < fire_burn_chance + fire_burn_per_tier × (tier - 1)

Catching fire, per tier5%
Added to the burn chance for each tier above 1.
Possessed monster turning50%
Each turn a possessed monster acts, this is the chance the spirit turns it on its own side.random < possess_chance: it attacks its own side
Monster changing stance25%
A two-element monster with no reason of its own to switch changes stance this often each turn. Bosses switch for their reasons instead.random < foe_stance_chance: it switches
Damage spread10%
Every hit is moved up or down by up to this much, so the same move never hits for exactly the same twice.damage × a random number between 1 - damage_jitter and 1 + damage_jitter
