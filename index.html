Nuitai

A tropical oceanic adventure where magic is kinesthetic.

A world being built for a turn-based RPG. Everything here is a work in progress.

I wanted to create a vast, bright, colorful water world of scattered islands with colossal wonders on the horizon, inspired by the nature, cultures and mythology of the Pacific.
