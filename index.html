Nuitai

An ocean world of scattered islands where magic is motion.

A world being built for a turn-based RPG. Everything here is a work in progress.

I wanted to create a vast, bright, beautiful and colorful water world that is mostly uncharted, unexplored and filled with colossal wonders that can be seen on the horizon. I wanted something with spiritual overtones and inspired by cultures that are under-represented in fantasy such as maritime Southeast Asia and Polynesia, and the philosophical and spiritual themes found in Buddhism, Taoism, animistic and nature religions like Shinto and the polytheistic beliefs of indigenous peoples.
